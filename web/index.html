02 / Sources & method

Observations,
not assumptions.

Road Pulse retains public DriveBC Open511 records, matching events by their source ID. Captures collect the configured feed every six hours; GitHub scheduling and source availability can delay them.

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – British Columbia. Independent CoolCode project; no government endorsement.

Read dates carefully

Observed means when this tool saved the feed. Source updated is DriveBC’s last modification time. Schedule ends and reopening estimates are plans, not proof that traffic resumed.

Disappearance is not reopening

“Active” can include future work. “Archived” can mean ceased, outdated or invalid. An event leaving a capture only proves it was absent from that observation. Changes are compared only when collection filters match.

Your roads, your collection

Use any road or location in the full BC capture. Saved filter views stay in this browser. Imports are processed locally and are not uploaded. Download receipts to keep them.

Change config.json to add quick searches, change API filters or bounds, and expand retention. A main-branch config change starts a new collection. Read the setup guide ↗
